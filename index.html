Safety First

TRACK PROTOCOLS & WAIVER

All drivers must present a valid, non-expired driver's license from their state or country of residence upon arrival. Drivers must be at least 18 years old. No prior circuit experience is required for GT Supercars; novice drivers receive mandatory telemetry & safety briefings.
Snell SA2020 or FIA 8859 approved full-face helmets and Hans devices are mandatory for all track sessions. Apex provides certified gear rentals, or drivers may bring their personal compliant racing gear after inspection by safety marshals.
Sessions run rain or shine with wet compound tires supplied for GT Supercars. In the event of severe lightning or flooding track closures, sessions will be rescheduled with zero fee penalty or fully credited.
